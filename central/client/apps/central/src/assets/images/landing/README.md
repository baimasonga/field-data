# Landing page photography

Drop field photographs here, then list them in
`src/components/landing-photos.js`. A photograph that is not listed there is
not shown, and every entry needs its own alt text, so nothing reaches the page
without a truthful description of what it shows.

## What to supply

- **Format** JPEG (photographs) — WebP also works if you would rather.
- **Size** 1600px on the long edge is plenty; the page never renders them
  larger than 800px wide on a 2x screen.
- **Aspect** 4:3 or 3:2 landscape. The grid crops to 4:3, so keep the subject
  away from the extreme edges.
- **Weight** aim under 300KB each after compression. These load on the same
  connections your enumerators work on.

## What makes a good one here

Show the work: an enumerator with a device and a respondent, in the setting the
survey covers — a household, a clinic, a water point, a school. Wide enough to
read the context, close enough to read the interaction.

## Before you publish a photograph of someone

Photographs of identifiable people need their permission to be used this way,
and a consent form signed for a survey does not cover marketing a platform.
Get explicit permission for public use, and be careful about photographing
people in circumstances they would not want shown — a clinic visit, for
instance. Where a face is not needed to tell the story, a wider frame or a
back-turned shot carries it without putting anyone on a public website.
