# F12: cross-form shared-identity peer backchecks

From an investigation's linked peer case, choose **Inspect linked case → Prepare targeted backcheck**. An active attached `identity-reused:*` finding can now offer neutral questions when its explicitly named related record is in this case's form, even if its primary record is in another form in the same project. Suggestions label **Cross-form peer record**. Dispatch and reviewed response-form generation use the linked peer case as their target.

Both forms must be live/readable; the primary record must be live and non-draft. Cross-form matches require an explicit matching `others[].xmlFormId` and `relatedInstanceId`. Missing or conflicting matching entries are excluded. Same-form fallback remains limited to the existing supported pair families. Cross-form similar-answer/location families are not enabled because their current generators do not establish that provenance. No form/peer identifiers, finding evidence or earlier answers enter suggestion payloads or generated prompts.

The instrument retains explicit human selection/editing, the 20-finding bound, published response-form choice, independent collector checks, current case and investigation revisions, active link and manager/source update permissions. Primary form read grants are checked and held during eligibility validation. New dispatch/form-generation requests revalidate mappings, source availability and both form permissions. Exact normalized retries may survive finding withdrawal, but selected finding source forms must still exist in the project and be readable. Revoking the primary source grant hides replay even if peer-source and response-form grants remain. Removed source findings also make replay unavailable. No fallback source, form or collector is chosen automatically.

Tests cover allowed cross-form identity mappings, permission filtering without identifiers, generated prompts, dispatch to the peer case, independent primary-source grant revocation, replay after withdrawal, missing/conflicting mappings and unsupported families. Existing instrument tests cover source/response permissions, revisions, concurrency, closure and link removal; browser tests cover scope labels, dispatch, retry/failure recovery, generated forms and mobile layout.

Broader peer families, multilingual/effectiveness evaluation, calibrated triage and field acceptance remain pending. Fraud intelligence and all eight areas remain partial.
