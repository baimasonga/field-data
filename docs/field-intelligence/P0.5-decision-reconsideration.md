# Reopening and reconsidering terminal decisions

A supervisor can reopen a resolved case for the current claim version from its
evidence panel. The action requires form `submission.update` and owning-project
`project.update`, a current `If-Match`, an idempotency key, and an explanation.
Permissions are checked again on every request, including successful replays.

Reopening uses the existing decisions endpoint with `outcome: needs-evidence`,
`override: true`, `reasonCode: reopen-for-review`, a nonblank note of at most
4,000 characters, and empty `evidenceIds`/`integrityFindingIds` arrays. It appends
a decision with fresh evidence/finding snapshots, clears assignment and
`resolvedAt`, sets the case to open, and increments its revision. It never edits
or deletes the earlier terminal decision. Only resolved cases with a terminal
head decision can be reopened; obsolete and superseded versions are refused.

The open case must be assigned again before a replacement terminal decision.
Ordinary reviewers may gather evidence, request independent backchecks or record
needs-evidence decisions. Any new acceptance or rejection after a prior terminal
decision requires supervisor permissions, `override: true`, the reason
`reconsidered-decision`, and a fresh explanation. Case detail exposes
`reversalRequired`; the evidence panel offers explicit replacement actions to
an assigned supervisor. A replacement may change or reaffirm the earlier outcome.

Replacement acceptance still requires at least one verified original and refuses
missing, unverified, mismatched or contradictory evidence. Both terminal outcomes
refuse pending backchecks. A supervisor may accept provenance limitations or
unresolved findings with their explanation; those limitations remain unchanged
and are preserved in the snapshot/audit context. The original
`verified-by-supervisor` reason cannot substitute for the reconsideration reason.

Every decision links to its immediate predecessor with an increasing sequence.
Reopening/reconsideration audits also identify the previous terminal decision.
Decision, case projection, audit and replay record commit atomically. Failed audits
roll everything back; retries return the original operation and revision without
reopening a case again after later decisions. Stale writes use the existing
decision conflict telemetry. Source provenance and legacy submission review state
remain independent of this history.

No schema migration is required. Workload metrics count reopening as an override
and show the case as open until reviewed again. Resolution time measures elapsed
time since the original case opening to its latest resolution; it is not a sum
of review-cycle durations. Rollback reverts application code while preserving all
appended decisions and audit events; do not delete history.

Real-transaction API coverage checks both reversal directions, snapshots, linked
history, permissions, obsolete versions, stale revisions, idempotency and audit
failure rollback. Browser fixtures verify explicit reopening/replacement controls
and ordinary reviewer restrictions. Device pilot results remain separate.
