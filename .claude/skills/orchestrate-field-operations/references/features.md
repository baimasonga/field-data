# Feature specifications

## Autonomous Sampling Controller

Continuously compare achieved observations with the approved sampling design. Identify undercoverage, nonresponse, attrition, and imbalance; propose statistically valid corrective assignments with weighting implications.

## Field Mission Autopilot

Plan daily missions using service times, travel estimates, skills, transport, capacity, deadlines, daylight, access windows, priority, and safety. Return route/assignment rationale and infeasibility explanations.

## Living Accessibility Map

Learn time-varying traversal information from consented operational traces and explicit field reports: mode, season, direction, travel time distribution, barriers, confidence, and freshness. Keep safety reports distinct from mere speed observations.

## Machine-Generated Field Assignments

Translate verified anomalies, expiring evidence, detected changes, or QA cases into actionable tasks. Include source, urgency, evidence request, eligibility, expiry, and closure criteria; deduplicate overlapping tasks.

## Adaptive Back-Check Allocation

Optimize independent back-check work for information gain subject to geography, cost, reviewer independence, safeguarding, and fairness. Protect against predictable patterns that can be gamed.
