# Build a Field Knowledge System

Transform disconnected submissions into a temporal, evidence-backed representation of real entities and decisions. Read [references/features.md](references/features.md) and [references/temporal-knowledge-contract.md](references/temporal-knowledge-contract.md).

## Workflow

1. Identify durable entities, claims, relationships, events, indicators, and decisions. Keep the original submission as source evidence.
2. Inspect identifiers, deduplication, longitudinal records, search, analytics, geospatial storage, permissions, and retention.
3. Define entity-resolution rules with match evidence, thresholds, manual merge/split, and reversibility.
4. Store bitemporal knowledge: when a fact was valid in reality and when the system learned or changed it.
5. Attach provenance and confidence to every derived fact, summary, and recommendation.
6. Compute freshness from fact type, volatility, events, source quality, and operational risk—not one global age threshold.
7. Turn expiry, change, anomaly, or missing knowledge into prioritized re-verification or action.
8. Evaluate whether the system improves decisions, not merely search relevance or dashboard volume.

## Knowledge safeguards

- Never collapse two people, households, places, or assets solely because names and coordinates are similar.
- Make merges reversible and preserve source identifiers.
- Separate “unknown,” “not observed,” “not applicable,” and “known false.”
- Provide source citations and validity dates for answers generated by institutional memory.
- Separate descriptive association, predictive estimates, and causal claims visibly.
- Do not display a digital twin as more current or complete than its evidence supports.
- Apply role and purpose controls to retrieval; search must not bypass record-level authorization.

## Architecture pattern

Use `source observation -> entity resolution -> claims/events -> temporal projections -> freshness/confidence -> query/analysis -> action -> new observation`. Build read models for search, maps, timelines, and analytics from authoritative events. Make projections rebuildable and versioned.

## Acceptance gate

Demonstrate creation, match suggestion, approved merge, correction, split, time-travel query, freshness decay, re-verification task, and source-cited knowledge answer. Test ambiguous identity, conflicting claims, late-arriving evidence, deleted/restricted sources, stale embeddings, projection rebuild, and permission changes.
