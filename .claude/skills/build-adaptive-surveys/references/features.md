# Feature specifications

## Adaptive Interview Intelligence

Select the next safe question from current answers, evidence coverage, uncertainty, burden budget, and measurement constraints. Use a deterministic policy graph for critical logic; allow models to propose within approved bounds.

## AI Survey Doctor

Parse a draft instrument and simulate diverse synthetic response paths to detect unreachable questions, cycles, contradictory constraints, excessive burden, missing translations, ambiguous wording, bias risks, and broken calculations. Produce reproducible findings tied to schema locations.

## Live Survey Evolution

Detect field friction and propose a versioned change. Route changes through review, compatibility assessment, pilot, staged release, monitoring, and rollback. Preserve earlier submissions under their original instrument.

## Answer Uncertainty

Attach provenance and uncertainty to an answer: exact recall, estimate, proxy respondent, enumerator observation, sensor-derived, media-derived, or imputed. Use suitable confidence or quality categories rather than inventing precision.

## Natural-Language Form Generation

Convert a manager’s intent into a draft schema, variable dictionary, labels, hints, constraints, skip logic, calculations, translations, and test cases. Require approval and static validation before deployment.

## Local-Language Conversational Prompts

Render canonical variables as approved conversational prompts while retaining exact data semantics. Support audio, script variations, fallback language, glossary locks, and enumerator confirmation for ambiguous recognition.
